Privacy Policy

Retrace  ·  Last updated: October 31, 2026

The short version: Your index stays on your device and the developer collects no data. Gemini handles indexing and search; chat goes directly to your selected AI provider. No Retrace backend is involved.

Overview

Retrace is a Chrome extension that lets you search and chat with your own browsing history. It is built with privacy as a first principle. No data is ever sent to the developer, and no backend server exists.

What Data the Developer Collects

None. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no server operated by this extension.

What Data the Extension Uses (Locally)

Your history index is stored in local IndexedDB. API keys are stored in Chrome's local extension storage. Content sent to AI providers is described below.

Browsing History

  • The extension reads the title, URL, and visit time of pages you visit.
  • This data is stored locally in IndexedDB and is never transmitted to the developer.

Page Content

  • The content script reads visible text from the page, plus selected image and audio URLs, to build a searchable index.
  • The extension stores an excerpt and up to roughly 30,000 characters of page text locally for indexing and search.
  • It does not intentionally read passwords or form values. Visible text on pages you visit, including signed-in pages, may be indexed.

Embeddings

  • Page text and selected media are sent directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API when indexing runs.
  • The resulting embeddings are stored locally in IndexedDB alongside the indexed page data.

Your API Keys

  • You provide your own Google Gemini API key in the extension settings.
  • The Gemini key is used for embeddings and semantic search, and for chat if you select Gemini.
  • If you select OpenAI or Anthropic for chat, you provide a separate key. Your chat prompt, conversation, and history-search results (including relevant page titles, URLs, and excerpts) are sent directly to that provider.
  • All keys are stored in chrome.storage.local. The developer never sees, receives, or stores them.
  • Keys in local browser storage are not encrypted by Retrace. Chrome profiles and extensions with sufficient access to your device may expose them.

Third-Party Services

AI requests go directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) for embeddings and semantic search. Chat requests go to your selected provider: Google Gemini, OpenAI (api.openai.com), or Anthropic (api.anthropic.com). Each provider handles data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Data Deletion

The history index lives in IndexedDB; API keys live in Chrome's local extension storage. Clear the index in Config and remove keys with Remove keys & sign out. Uninstalling the extension or clearing its site data also removes local extension data. Retrace does not retain a remote copy. Data sent to AI providers is handled under their policies and terms.

Children's Privacy

This extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

Any changes will be published in the extension's repository with a new effective date. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
